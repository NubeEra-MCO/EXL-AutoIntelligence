## How to Create a Request

### Step 1: Search for Your Policy

1. Click **"Create New Request"**
2. Enter your **Policy Number** (format: INS-XXXX-XXXXX)
3. Click **"Search"**
4. Your policy details will appear:
   - Policy holder name
   - Current status (Active/Inactive/Suspended/Lapsed)
   - Coverage type
   - Current address
   - Premium information

### Step 2: Choose Request Type

Select one of these request types:

#### 📍 Address Change
- **What:** Update your registered address
- **When:** You're moving or need to correct your address
- **Required Info:** New complete address, verification ID
- **Time:** Usually 2 business days
- **Approval:** Not required

#### 👤 Nominee Change  
- **What:** Update who receives the policy benefits
- **When:** Family changes (marriage, birth, divorce, etc.)
- **Required Info:** New nominee name, relationship, ID proof
- **Time:** Usually 3 business days
- **Approval:** Required (manager review)
- **Note:** Your policy must be Active with no outstanding claims

#### 💰 Premium Mode Change
- **What:** Change how often you pay (Monthly → Quarterly → Annual)
- **When:** You want different payment frequency
- **Required Info:** New payment mode, effective date
- **Time:** Usually 5 business days  
- **Approval:** Required
- **Note:** Only available for Annual policies with good payment history

#### 📋 Information Update
- **What:** Update other policy information (email, phone, coverage details)
- **When:** Any other information needs updating
- **Required Info:** What to update and new value
- **Time:** Usually 1 business day
- **Approval:** Not required

### Step 3: Fill in Request Details

1. Fill in all **required fields** (marked with *)
2. Provide clear, complete information
3. Add any helpful notes in the comments section
4. Review your information carefully

**Example for Address Change:**
```
NEW ADDRESS: 456 Oak Avenue, Chicago, IL 60601
VERIFICATION ID: Driver's License: IL-D1234567
REASON: Relocated for work
NOTES: Same phone number, only address changed
```

### Step 4: Check Eligibility

1. Click **"Check Eligibility"**
2. Wait for the system to verify your policy
3. You'll see one of these results:

**✅ ELIGIBLE** - Your request can proceed
- Click **"Submit Request"** to continue
- Request status becomes "Submitted"

**❌ NOT ELIGIBLE** - Your policy doesn't meet requirements
- See the reason why (e.g., "Policy is Suspended")
- You may need to contact customer service
- You cannot submit until your policy status changes

### Step 5: Submit Your Request

1. Review all information one final time
2. Click **"Submit Request"**
3. You'll see a confirmation message with your **Request ID**
4. **Save this ID** - you'll use it to track your request

**Example Request ID:** REQ-2024-00156

## How to Track Your Request

### Method 1: In the App
1. Click **"Track Request"** on home screen
2. Select your request from the list, or
3. Search by **Policy Number** or **Request ID**
4. Click the request to see full details

### Method 2: Using the AI Assistant
1. Click **"Chat with Assistant"**
2. Ask: "What's the status of my request?"
3. Provide your Request ID or Policy Number
4. Assistant will show current status and history

### What You'll See

**Request Card shows:**
- Request ID
- Request type
- Current status with color indicator
- When it was created
- Expected completion date
- Assigned handler (if any)

**Status Timeline shows:**
- Every status change over time
- When each change happened
- Brief notes about each step
- Who handled it

**Example Timeline:**
```
Sep 1, 10:30am - Created (You submitted the request)
Sep 1, 10:40am - Eligible (Eligibility check passed)
Sep 2, 8:00am - In Progress (Assigned to John)
```

## Request Status Guide

### Status Meanings

| Status | Icon | Meaning | What's Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | 📝 | You're still preparing it | Finish filling info & submit |
| **Submitted** | 📤 | We received it | Wait for eligibility check (usually minutes) |
| **Eligible** | ✓ | Your policy qualifies | We're processing it |
| **Approved** | 👍 | Manager approved | We're finalizing the change |
| **Completed** | ✓✓ | Done! Change applied | Check your policy details |

| **On Hold** | ⏸ | Waiting for info | We may have contacted you |
| **Cancelled** | ⊗ | Cancelled | Request won't be processed |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How do I know if my request was received?**
A: You'll get an email confirmation immediately after submitting. You'll also see your Request ID on screen.

**Q: Can I cancel a request?**
A: Yes, contact customer service with your Request ID. Requests in "Draft" or "Submitted" status can be easily cancelled.

**Q: What if I made a mistake in my request?**
A: If it's still in "Draft" status, you can edit it. Once submitted, contact support for modifications.

**Q: Why was my request rejected?**
A: Scroll down in the request details to see the rejection reason. Common reasons:
- Policy is Suspended or Lapsed (must be Active for most requests)
- Policy has outstanding claims (nominee change)
- Policy is not Annual mode (premium mode change)
- Too many payment failures (premium mode change)

### Eligibility Questions

**Q: When am I eligible to change my address?**
A: Your policy must be Active or Inactive (not Suspended or Lapsed).

**Q: Can I change my nominee?**
A: Only if your policy is Active AND you have no outstanding claims.

**Q: How do I change my premium payment frequency?**
A: Your policy must be Annual and you must have no payment failures in the last 12 months.

### Timeline Questions

**Q: How long does my request take?**
A: Depends on the request type:
- Address Change: 2 business days
- Nominee Change: 3 business days (requires approval)
- Premium Mode: 5 business days (requires approval & backend processing)
- Info Update: 1 business day

**Q: What are "business days"?**
A: Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. Weekends and holidays don't count.

**Q: Why is my request taking longer?**
A: Common reasons:
- Approval is pending (manager review)
- Additional verification needed
- High volume of requests
- Missing information

### Notification Questions

**Q: How will I know when my request is done?**
A: You'll receive an email notification at each major status change. You can also check the app anytime.

**Q: Can I change my notification preferences?**
A: Go to account settings (gear icon in top right) and adjust notification options.

### Chat Assistant Questions

**Q: What can the AI assistant help with?**
A: The assistant can:
- Tell you your request status
- Answer questions about policy servicing
- Help you start a new request
- Provide information about eligibility
- Direct you to customer service if needed

**Q: How do I use the assistant?**
A: Click the chat icon and type your question. The assistant learns from context, so you can ask follow-up questions naturally.

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't find my policy"
- Verify you're entering the correct policy number (format: INS-XXXX-XXXXX)
- Check if you're logged into the correct account
- Policy may be inactive - call customer service

### "The eligibility check failed"
- See the eligibility message for the reason
- Most common: Policy is Suspended or Lapsed
- Contact your agent to update policy status

### "I get an error when submitting"
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page and try again
- If problem persists, contact support

### "I don't see my request in the list"
- Refresh your browser (Ctrl+R or F5)
- Try searching by Request ID instead
- It may still be processing - wait a few minutes

### "I didn't get my confirmation email"
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify your email address in settings
- Resend confirmation via the app
